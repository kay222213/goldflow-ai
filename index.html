Calculation formulas
  • Monetary risk = current equity × risk percentage ÷ 100.
  • Equity drawdown = max(0, equity peak − current equity); drawdown % = drawdown ÷ peak × 100.
  • Remaining daily loss = max(0, daily maximum loss + today's realized P/L) when P/L is negative; this simple tracker does not include unentered open-position exposure.
  • Required wins are hypothetical: target ÷ assumed net profit per winning trade. They do not imply a forecast.
  • Position sizing is intentionally withheld unless instrument contract size, tick value, stop distance, and conversion are verified.

Data sources & settings

PROVIDER-INDEPENDENT

This GitHub Pages build has no secret-bearing backend. Public APIs frequently require a key, impose CORS/rate limits, or restrict client-side calls. If no provider works, the app remains honestly disconnected. Do not put private keys in this repository.

What the data can and cannot tell you

  • Retail spot XAUUSD quotes: broker-specific OTC bid/ask stream; not a consolidated global tape. Tick direction is not equivalent to exchange aggressor classification.
  • Public candle/quote APIs: useful for indicative price context when accessible, but availability, delay, history, CORS, and licensing vary. This build does not fabricate a live price when access fails.
  • COMEX GC/MGC futures: exchange-traded volume and aggressor-side data may support genuine futures order-flow analysis from a suitable feed. Futures are not spot XAUUSD; compare only after calculating a timestamp-aligned basis (futures − spot), accounting for contract expiry and roll.
  • CVD / footprint / DOM / absorption: disabled unless a compatible feed supplies classified trades or order-book events. Candle volume and candle direction are not a substitute.
  • Sessions: session windows must be interpreted in a timezone-aware market calendar; daylight-saving changes affect London/New York. This static build labels times conservatively and avoids asserting precise session levels without adequate candles.
